# Overview

This roadmap defines the strategic evolution of CyberShield Range over a 24-month period.

The objective is to progressively transform CyberShield Range from a conceptual architecture into a fully operational cyber range capable of supporting cybersecurity education, attack simulation, threat detection and incident response.
